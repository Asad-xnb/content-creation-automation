# ReceptraLink Content Doctrine

**Brand Identity**: ReceptraLink is an AI voice receptionist that handles inbound business calls, answers customer questions, collects information, schedules appointments, and automates routine workflows. It helps hospitals, clinics, real estate businesses, and other service organizations reduce missed calls, improve response times, and operate more efficiently.

**Target Audience**: 
Service-based business owners (clinics, hospitals, real estate agencies, home services, law firms, dental practices). We are writing to the business owner who is tired of missing calls after hours, tired of paying for expensive answering services, and looking for operational efficiency through AI.

**Core Themes to AMPLIFY**:
- The hidden cost of missed inbound calls in service businesses.\n- Highly informative marketing solutions and growth strategies for service businesses.\n- Engaging, hook-driven educational content about operational scale and marketing automation.
- How AI voice agents differ from terrible legacy IVR phone trees ("Press 1 for...").
- Real-world math: human receptionist vs AI voice agent vs offshore call center.
- Scheduling automation and CRM integration via voice.
- The future of front-desk operations in clinics and real estate.

**Themes to DROP (Banned)**:
- Generic "future of work" for generalists.
- SaaS metrics (MRR, churn, onboarding flows, paywalls).
- Indie-hacker coding tactics or "build in public".
- B2B cold email / outbound lead generation tactics (ReceptraLink handles INBOUND calls, not outbound cold calling).
- Generic ChatGPT prompts.

**Carousel Guidance**:
Carousels should ALWAYS focus on inbound call strategies, the cost of missed calls, or how AI voice agents streamline operations.
*Example hooks*: "Hospitals miss 34% of calls. Here's the math.", "The $50,000 cost of a missed real estate lead.", "Why your answering service is killing your brand."

**Voice & Tone**:
Professional, empathetic, data-driven, and highly operational. We speak to business owners like a sharp COO. No hype-bro language, no generic marketing speak.
